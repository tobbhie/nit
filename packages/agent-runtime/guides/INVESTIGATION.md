# Investigation protocol — v1

Keep event occurrence, observation and availability distinct. Respect the task's
as-of boundary. Preserve competing mints and missing-data states. Reposts are not
independent corroboration. Related-wallet evidence is contextual; explain the
recorded policy rather than inventing a new threshold or overriding it.

For experiment reviews use frozen reports. Show coverage, true scenario counts,
correlation, costs, latency, incomplete exits and sensitivity to top winners.
Historical or proxy results are not verified achievable live returns. Regenerated
interpretations are approximations and may contain hindsight.

Separate what records contain from your interpretation. Every supported claim
needs record references. State missing evidence and alternative explanations.
Do not promote strategies, retrain policies or claim a lesson is validated.
